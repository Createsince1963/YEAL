Quick start

A normal download needs only a link, a format, and one click on Download.

  1. Start Yeal with Run_Yeal.bat.
  2. Paste a video or playlist link into the link field, or open the video in the embedded YouTube browser.
  3. Select Video or Audio (MP3) and the profile you want.
  4. Optionally enable the description, transcript, online player, lyrics, or album tracks.
  5. Click Download and answer any selection dialogs.
Note: Opening a video in the browser only copies its link into the workflow. Nothing downloads until you confirm it.

The interface

Downloads / Workflow

Link field, format, profile, optional extras, Download button, and the grouped job list.

Profiles

Reusable presets for quality, format, subtitles, metadata, lyrics, and album handling.

Settings

Browser, appearance, target folder, speed, sign-in, proxy, yt-dlp, and advanced options.

YouTube browser

Displayed beside the workflow on Windows. Swap sides or drag the divider to resize it.

When the window becomes narrow, Yeal automatically changes to compact navigation at the bottom.

Downloading video or audio

Add links

  • Type a link, paste it from the clipboard, or drop it onto the Yeal window.
  • You may paste several HTTP(S) links at once.
  • The embedded browser recognizes the current video, Short, live stream, or playlist automatically.

Video

Select a video profile and maximum resolution. When video and audio arrive as separate streams, Yeal needs ffmpeg to merge them.

Optional content

A profile can save the description as HTML, transcript as TXT, a player page, lyrics, and album tracks. Missing optional content never fails the main download.

Slow connection: Use one parallel download and set a speed limit so that the embedded player retains enough bandwidth.

Audio, MP3, and tags

Yeal creates MP3 files at 320 kbps. If a source exceeds normal streaming quality, a dialog can also offer AAC/M4A, the original stream, and FLAC for a lossless source.

  • MP3: broadest compatibility and Yeal's recommended option.
  • AAC/M4A: good quality without MP3 re-encoding when a suitable stream exists.
  • Original: keeps the supplied audio stream unchanged.
  • FLAC: useful only when the source itself is lossless.

After an MP3 download, Yeal may ask whether to edit the automatically discovered tags. You can change title, artist, album, genre, year, mood, keywords, and comment. “No” is the default; automatic tags remain in the file either way.

Playlists, albums, and live streams

Playlists

A playlist link opens an item list. Choose individual entries, use Shift + click for a range, or Ctrl + A for all. Numbered files and an HTML playlist are saved under Playlists\Name.

Albums from timestamps

When Yeal recognizes a track list in the description, choose “Tracks only,” “Tracks + full file,” or “No thanks.” Split tracks receive names, album and track numbers, and an M3U8 playlist.

Live streams

For a live stream, choose recording until the end, 15/30 minutes, 1/2 hours, or a custom duration. Time limits require ffmpeg. You can cancel an active recording in the download list.

Embedded YouTube browser

On Windows, Yeal uses Microsoft Edge WebView2; it does not bundle another browser. Google may refuse account sign-in in embedded browsers.

  • Back, Forward, Reload, and YouTube Home sit above the web page.
  • “Open in your browser” opens the current page in the normal system browser.
  • “Use this link” copies the current address into the workflow again.
  • The browser button shows or hides the page; the stack button swaps the sides.
  • Drag the divider to change the saved width.
  • The page follows Yeal's light or dark theme.
  • Ads are muted. Yeal automatically presses visible “Skip,” “Skip ad,” and “Überspringen” controls.
Age-restricted or protected videos: Sign in to YouTube in Firefox, Edge, or Chrome, select the same browser under “Settings > Browser login,” close that browser, and retry the download. The sign-in inside Yeal's embedded browser is not used for downloads.
Ad blocking: This feature is experimental and can interfere with YouTube. Disable it and restart Yeal if the site behaves unexpectedly.

TIDAL

TIDAL needs your own TIDAL subscription. Yeal uses the helper tiddl for downloads and the sign-in you create in Settings > Tidal; it never sees your password.

Three views

The buttons above the workflow switch the right-hand side: YouTube, TIDAL Web (the embedded TIDAL web player, where you sign in yourself) and TIDAL DIY (Yeal's own TIDAL view). Only one of them plays at a time; the one you leave is paused.

TIDAL DIY

  • Pages: Start, Explore, Feed and My collection (playlists, albums, artists, tracks, mixes, videos), search, album, artist, playlist and mix pages.
  • Playing: Yeal's own player with queue, shuffle, repeat, volume, lyrics and a quality badge that shows what really plays (with the tiddl sign-in TIDAL plays at most CD quality; downloads are not affected).
  • Queue: drag the tiles to change the order, “Save as playlist” stores it in your TIDAL account.
  • Playlists: create, rename, delete, add and remove titles, add titles to your collection with the heart.
  • Right-click on any tile, row or playlist: play, play next, add to queue, collection, add to playlist, download, save as HTML link list, copy link, open in TIDAL Web.
  • HTML link list: saves a page with links to the titles of a playlist, album or mix without downloading anything (folder Tidal\Linklisten in the download folder; an existing page is never replaced).

Arranging the view

Playlists and “Search and queue” can be docked on the left or right, detached into their own window or hidden (button in the header of the area, or right-click). The two buttons next to the page tabs show or hide them, the third resets everything. The player bar moves to the top or bottom with a right-click. Yeal remembers the arrangement; Settings > Tidal > TIDAL DIY layout resets it.

Track player

Right-click the player bar and choose Track player (oval) (or pick it in Settings > Tidal > TIDAL DIY layout). The player becomes a running track: the dot is the playhead and runs once around while the song plays; click or drag on the loop to jump. The green band around the inner oval shows the level of the music (the louder, the thicker; VIS switches it off). VOL shows the volume as a big number and turns the band yellow; the mouse wheel over the oval changes it too. Under the oval: repeat (off / all / single), shuffle, like, PLAYLIST (queue, playlists, search results) and LYRICS. An equalizer is not built in.

Player for your downloads

The fourth button above the workflow, Player, is Yeal's own player for the songs you downloaded: the same oval, with the library of all finished audio downloads (also the songs inside downloaded albums and playlists) and a filter field. You can also right-click a download and choose Play in Yeal player. STREAM plays the current song on the speaker or TV chosen under Stream to box (the song then stops here). The player has its own volume, and it never plays together with the other players (switch it off in Settings under single playback).

The sun and its planets

In the player for your downloads the oval is the sun in the middle; around it, on the orbits, are the planets: Library (left), Queue (right), Played (below), Settings and Speaker (above). A click unfolds a planet into a card and folds it away again; the sun and its orbits move over to make room, so nothing is covered. The sun button in the corner folds all planets into the sun. Settings has the player colour (green, blue, red, yellow, purple, orange, pink, cyan, white; also the COLOR button under the oval), the band around the oval, shuffle, repeat and volume. Speaker lists the speakers and TVs and plays the current song there (Stream to box). Under the oval you always see volume and a 10-second jump back / ahead.

Shaping the orbits

The slider Orbit in the corner of the sun view (or the mouse wheel on the empty sky) makes all orbits larger or smaller; the sun grows with the window. Drag a planet to move it around the sun or in and out: it gets its own orbit, and snaps onto an orbit that is already used when you let go close to it. The round arrow button puts all planets back on the main orbit and the slider back to the middle. Everything is remembered.

TIDAL DIY as a sun view

The sun button next to the page tabs of TIDAL DIY switches the view: the player is the sun in the middle, and your playlists (left), the queue (right), the songs played and the settings (below), the search and the lyrics (above) are the planets. Titles, albums and playlists dropped on the sun play at once. The house button in the corner leads back to the normal pages.

Bluetooth headphones and other outputs of this computer

Windows lists Bluetooth headphones, HDMI monitors and wired speakers as audio outputs. In the Speaker planet of the player for downloads the button THIS PC shows them as moons around the planet; in the sun view of TIDAL DIY the planet Bluetooth does the same. A click on a moon switches the players to that output at once, also while a song plays. Pair and switch on the headphones in Windows first; if the same headphones appear twice, choose the stereo entry (not “Hands-Free”) for the best sound. WLAN shows the speakers and TVs of the network for Stream to box.

Seamless change, level band, glow, outer ring, own colours

In the Settings planet: Band strength (50-300 %) makes the level band around the oval higher or flatter - the quiet and loud parts of a song are stretched over the whole range, so the band is no longer a flat ring. Glow (0-300 %) sets the glow around the inner lane; it swells with the loud parts. Outer ring shows how far the whole playlist, album, folder or queue has played. Seamless change (off, 5, 8 or 10 s) lets the next song fade in while the old one fades out; a second ring in its own lane runs from 5 s before the change to 5 s after it began. Under the colours you can make up to 5 own colours with the + button (right click or long press removes one). The volume of the player is separate from the Windows volume: both work together, the sound is as loud as both allow.

The own audio engine: equalizer, crossfeed, ReplayGain, convolution

The Sound planet (in the sun views) switches between the Standard player and Yeal's Own engine. The own engine decodes with ffmpeg, filters the sound in 32-bit float and plays it through the output you chose (also Bluetooth headphones: Windows keeps the connection, Yeal only does the maths). It offers: a 10 band equalizer with pre-amp and presets (linear phase, so the sound is not smeared), crossfeed for headphones (light / medium / strong), ReplayGain (every song about equally loud; TIDAL songs use TIDAL's own value, downloaded files are measured), convolution with an impulse response in a WAV file (room or headphone correction) and a soft limiter that prevents clipping when filters raise the level. Needs numpy and ffmpeg (not part of the exe unless added); without them the Standard player stays. Filters add a short delay (about 40 ms with the equalizer); the position display takes it into account. Switching the engine does not stop the song: the new engine takes over at the same place while the old one fades out.

Drag titles into the queue

Titles, albums and playlists can be dragged from the pages into the queue (the line shows where they land). Dragging a selected title takes the whole selection with it. Dropped on the player itself (Drop to play) they replace the queue and play at once.

Download by drag and drop

Drag a title, album, playlist or mix from TIDAL DIY onto the workflow panel: it is added to the downloads at once. Links dragged there from other places fill the link field as before.

TIDAL options

The option groups are small tiles in two columns, each with its current value; click one to open its choices below (one group at a time). A click on the heading “TIDAL options” folds the whole block into one line. The groups: quality, videos, video quality, Dolby Atmos, artist singles and extras (lyrics, .lrc, cover, M3U, player page, overwrite, rewrite tags). The values are the defaults for every download and are also found in Settings > Tidal. Downloads run in Yeal's own tiddl folder, your own tiddl setup is not changed.

Queue and download list

Jobs are grouped by date. Narrow the list with All, Active, Queued, Done, and Failed filters or the search field.

  • Cancel: stops a queued or running job.
  • Retry: starts a failed or cancelled job again.
  • Remove: removes the list entry, not necessarily the saved file.
  • Details: shows the path, status, and technical error.
  • Show in folder / Play in VLC: available for completed files.

For a known link, Yeal asks whether to download it again or open the existing file.

Using profiles

Profiles store frequently used combinations. They can include format, quality cap, audio format, subtitles, playlist behavior, metadata, album splitting, and lyrics.

  1. Open the Profiles page.
  2. Select an existing profile or create a new one.
  3. Configure it, assign a clear name, and save it.
  4. Select the profile from the workflow.

Extracting images from video

This desktop feature requires ffmpeg. Open it with the grid button in the workflow.

  1. Select a downloaded video or drop a video file from Explorer.
  2. Choose start and end with the red handles or enter time values.
  3. Select an interval and PNG or JPG.
  4. Press “Create images.”

Images go into a dedicated subfolder under Frames. Intervals below one second are available only for Shorts up to three minutes.

Important settings

On first start, Yeal asks for the browser used by protected YouTube downloads (Chrome is preselected) and the download folder. Click a Settings heading to expand or collapse that complete section. Changes take effect only after Save; leaving offers Save, Discard, or Stay. There is no prompt when nothing changed.

Files and storage locations

  • Video\ – video downloads
  • Audio\ – audio and MP3 downloads
  • Playlists\Name\ – selected playlist items
  • Frames\Video\ – extracted still images

Yeal can offer newly connected USB or external drives as targets and displays free and total space. The last 500 completed jobs remain in history.

Troubleshooting

Video buffers while a download is active

Set parallel downloads to 1 and choose a download rate below the available connection speed. This controls bandwidth, not the YouTube player's own buffer.

A video restarts after a dialog

This is not ordinary network buffering. Yeal must not recreate the browser view while a dialog is open. Test with the current Python source and report which dialog triggered it.

YouTube downloads do not work

In Settings, confirm that yt-dlp is current and a JavaScript runtime (Node.js or Deno) is available. Optional browser login may help with age- or member-restricted content.

Audio and video are not merged

ffmpeg must be available. Without it, Yeal can save only formats already delivered as a single file.

Rate limit or bot check

Keep Gentle mode enabled and wait. Yeal pauses the queue automatically. Repeated immediate retries can extend the restriction.

Missing file or full drive

Check the target folder, free disk space, and write permissions. For external drives, confirm that Windows still assigned the same drive letter.

Security, privacy, and legal

  • Saved YouTube/browser credentials are kept in the Admin vault and protected on Windows by DPAPI plus a password-derived key. A new vault starts with 1234; this public initial password must be replaced with a private password before entries become available.
  • The vault locks after five minutes; copied secrets are cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds.
  • The local HTML preview binds only to 127.0.0.1.
  • Download only content for which you have permission or the necessary usage rights.
